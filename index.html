About Me

Is That All? Certainly not.

There is more to it than just that. I have actually completed quite a few projects, but since I kept them in Google Docs and because downloading or transferring files took too much time I don't have photos or documentation of the final results. As for my skills and activities, I engage in painting and Taekwondo. Painting is really fun and creative I used to mix mediums like crayons, colored pencils, and markers. With Taekwondo, the exciting part is kicking or breaking the boards. Regarding the projects I’ve created specifically in robotics the list includes a traffic light system with a push button mechanism and actual lights, as well as a planetary model featuring moving or illuminated parts. I also developed simple games using BuildBox, Scratch, and Roblox Studio, along with various apps—such as shopping apps, to-do lists, and CRUD applications built using Thunkable, Figma, and Spreets. Looking back, the time I spent at home during 9th grade and my elementary school years was really exciting, and these activities became genuine hobbies of mine. Even now, after a long break, I’ve picked things up again; I really enjoy creating stories using the CapCut app and Gacha Nebula characters, and I also edit videos using Alight Motion.
